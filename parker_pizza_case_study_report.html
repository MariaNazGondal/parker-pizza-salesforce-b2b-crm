1. Executive Summary & Business Context

Parker Pizza initiated a strategic expansion to place its frozen gourmet pizza products onto regional grocery store shelves. Unlike direct-to-consumer retail, B2B grocery distribution requires managing protracted sales cycles, negotiating margin agreements, coordinating sampling presentations, and delivering post-signing merchandising collateral.

Identified Pain Points

  • Fragmented Accounts: Store branch locations and procurement buyer histories were tracked across spreadsheets without relationship hierarchies.
  • Multi-Stakeholder Friction: B2B grocery deals required consensus across Category Managers, Store Directors, and Merchandisers.
  • Disconnected Hand-offs: Point-of-Sale (POS) sampling logistics had zero automated trigger upon contract finalization.
  • Pipeline Blindspots: Leadership lacked unified visibility into projected wholesale revenues and stage-by-stage deal velocity.

Solution Objectives

  • Unified 360° Data Model: Model store accounts, purchasing contacts, and marketing assets within native Salesforce objects.
  • Standardized Sales Lifecycle: Deploy a guided 5-stage opportunity pipeline with validation rules and key field prompts.
  • Automated Revenue Operations: Deploy Flows for opportunity naming standards, executive margin alerts, and task generation.
  • Role-Based Governance: Implement granular profiles, permission sets, and field-level security reflecting enterprise standards.

3. Relational Data Model & Schema Design

The schema was built to support complex retail grocery dynamics while avoiding unnecessary technical overhead. Ten custom objects and fields were implemented to balance referential integrity and reporting agility.

Schema Builder: Object Relationships & Junctions
DATA INTEGRITY MODEL
Architectural Decisions

Rather than storing retail branch data flatly, relationships were designed for long-term scalability:

  • Account Hierarchy: Supermarket chains (e.g., Salling Group, Coop) configured as parent accounts with child accounts representing individual retail locations.
  • Lookup Relationships: Connected wholesale delivery points and distributor brokers to Opportunities without cascading deletions.
  • Master-Detail Relationships: Tied custom In-Store Promotional Campaigns to Accounts to enforce roll-up summary reporting on marketing investments.

5. Process Automation via Flow Builder

Following modern Salesforce best practices, workflow rules and process builders were completely avoided in favor of three modular Record-Triggered Flows:

Flow Builder: Business Automation Canvas
LOW-CODE REVOPS ENGINE
3 Automated Flows Configured
  • Standardized Naming Convention: Automatically formats Opportunity titles as [Account Name] - [Product SKU] - [Close Quarter] to prevent search chaos.
  • Financial Threshold Notification: When an opportunity value exceeds $50,000 in annual volume, an automated notification and chatter alert routes to RevOps executives.
  • Post-Signing Merchandising Task Creation: Moving a deal to Closed-Won automatically generates onboarding tasks for the field support team with a 5-day due date.

6. Security, Access & Compliance Discipline

Drawing from 5 years of operations leadership at Pakistan's largest commercial bank, enterprise governance standards were incorporated to ensure data privacy, auditability, and least-privilege access.

Enterprise Governance in Practice

"In commercial banking operations, data silos and improper data access directly compromise compliance. For Parker Pizza, the same rigor was applied: standardizing access through Profiles and Permission Sets to ensure sales teams could close deals rapidly without exposing wholesale discount limits or executive margins."

Profiles & Role Hierarchy

  • 3 Custom Profiles: B2B Sales Representative, Marketing Specialist, and RevOps Executive.
  • 4 Hierarchical Roles: Structured reporting paths ensure regional managers automatically inherit record visibility from field territory reps.
  • Organization-Wide Defaults (OWD): Accounts and Opportunities set to Private, opening access conditionally via sharing rules.

Modular Permission Sets & FLS

  • 6 Permission Sets: Enabled modular capabilities (e.g., Campaign Creation, Discount Approval Overrides) without profile proliferation.
  • Field-Level Security (FLS): Sensitive financial fields like "Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)" and "Wholesale Margin %" restricted to RevOps leadership.
  • Audit Logging: Field History Tracking enabled on key contract negotiation fields.
